Screen Ruler

More info & support the dev

Why I made it

I’m a woodworker and a coder and I can't ever seem to find a ruler outside my workshop. So I made one, Screen Ruler is a straightforward ruler, some roughly accurate calipers, a perfectly cromulent protractor, and a Bubble Level I'm actually rather proud of. The big thing is the ruler that can be calibrated against the screen already in your hand.

Privacy & planned ads

Screen Ruler does not collect personal information: it does not ask for your name, email address, account, contacts or precise location, and its calibration preferences stay in your browser. If you choose the optional Online Device Match, the browser-provided device model hint and screen dimensions are sent to the matching service solely to identify the screen. There are no ads on the site today, but I plan to place a Google ad banner at the bottom to help pay for it. When that happens, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, IP addresses or similar identifiers for ad delivery and measurement; Google explains those practices in its advertising privacy information.

How it works

Screen Ruler turns the screen’s pixel dimensions and a selected display diagonal into a calibrated scale. Its local phone presets are curated from openly licensed device-specification data, including the Global Smartphone Database 2025, alongside public manufacturer specifications and model-code aliases. It tries the model hint supplied by your browser first; if that does not identify a device, the optional Online Device Match can use MobileAPI as a third-party fallback. You can always select a device or calibrate manually instead.
