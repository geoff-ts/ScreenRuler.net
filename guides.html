Measuring guides

Notes from a woodworker who keeps needing a ruler when the workshop is somewhere else. Each guide covers one job you can do with a screen in your hand, and is honest about where a screen stops being the right tool.

  • Everyday objects with known sizes

    Bank cards, paper and coins whose dimensions are fixed by a standard or a mint, and which of them make a trustworthy calibration reference.

  • Identifying screws, bolts and drill bits

    Read a metric thread size, count the pitch, convert a wood-screw gauge and match a drill bit, all with the calipers on your screen.

  • Inch fractions to millimetres

    A conversion table in 1/32" steps, how to read a fractional rule, and when to round to the nearest 1/16".

  • Why a browser can't tell how big your screen is

    Pixels, pixel density, CSS pixels and device pixel ratio, and why every on-screen ruler needs calibrating.

Using the ruler itself

  • How to use Screen Ruler

    Set up your screen, then use the calipers, protractor and bubble level, with troubleshooting.

  • Accuracy and calibration

    Calibrate against a known length, check both axes and understand read precision versus physical accuracy.
