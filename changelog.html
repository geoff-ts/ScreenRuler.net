What's new

Screen Ruler is a working project. This page lists what has changed, newest first, so you can see what's different since your last visit.

2 October 2026

  • New measuring guides: everyday objects with known sizes, identifying screws, bolts and drill bits, inch fractions to millimetres and why a browser can't tell how big your screen is.
  • Added this page.

22 September 2026

  • Added an illustrated how-to guide and an accuracy and calibration guide.
  • Added a privacy page and rewrote the about and contact pages.
  • The introduction and guides now work with JavaScript turned off, and the ruler recovers if a script loads slowly or a third-party service fails.
  • The device dialog now closes with the Escape key and returns focus to the button that opened it.

13 August 2026

  • Faster, more reliable start-up, and preferences keep working when browser storage is blocked.

1 August 2026

  • Experimental BIG RULER AR mode, which uses the camera on compatible Android devices to measure distances in a room.
  • Calipers that are dragged off screen now show a marker at the edge so you can find them again. Larger, easier handles.
  • Screen-reader labels throughout the controls.

24 July 2026

  • The ruler and calipers stay put when you rotate the device.

15 July 2026

  • Shared calibrations: if you calibrate a device that isn't in the list, you can choose to share its dimensions so the next person with that phone doesn't have to.
  • Redesigned settings panel.

14 July 2026

  • Tablet presets and the bubble level.
  • Smarter device matching and an about page.

13 July 2026

  • First release: an on-screen ruler with calipers, a protractor, a grid and nearly 400 phone display presets.
