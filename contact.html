Help me reproduce a measuring problem

A useful report describes what happened and what you expected. Include:

  • Your device model, browser and operating system.
  • Whether you were in portrait or landscape, and whether browser zoom or display scaling had changed.
  • The selected device preset or manual calibration values.
  • The length of your reference object and the reading shown by the ruler.
  • The steps that reproduce the problem, and a screenshot if it helps.

Please do not include passwords, advertising account credentials or private device identifiers. Check the troubleshooting guide first for scale and sensor problems.
